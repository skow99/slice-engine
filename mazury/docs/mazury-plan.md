# Mazury prototype milestones

Lake Niegocin uses surveyed geography. The yacht and harbour manoeuvres use metres and seconds. Open-water travel is compressed for shorter sessions. The prototype is reviewed one playable milestone at a time. [Sailing research](mazury-sailing-research.md)

| Milestone | Playable review |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Scale and Ekomarina | Approach in a 7.35 m yacht, bring it inside one traced berth using ahead and astern, moor in neutral, step onto the pontoon, and board again. Compare the close and wide cameras, try the open-water exit, and check staged sail movement and keyboard shortcuts. |
| 2. Wind, rig and handling | Raise and lower the **grot** and **fok** independently. Check sail position, speed, leeway, steering authority and heel in a steady wind. |
| 3. Tacks and gybes | Complete **zwrot przez sztag** and **zwrot przez rufę** through the wind. Check a slow tack that stalls and a gybe where the boom changes sides. |
| 4. Gusts and guidance | See a gust travel across the water and feel the boat respond. Select a target and use the heading, track and Polish sailing cues to steer towards it. |
| 5. Additional ports | Apply the berth and departure checks to Wilkasy and Rydzewo. Test the continuous sailing legs between their mapped approaches. |
| 6. Lake traffic | Set traffic to 0, 5 and 30 boats from the top bar. Watch crews cast off, tack, stop for lunch, and head for an evening berth. Send in rain and a thunderstorm, jump the clock to night, and watch the boats settle. Check early diversions, stern-to berthing and crossing traffic. Drag the camera in both views and return to the yacht. |
| 7. Crew balance and changing weather | Shift crew weight at anchor and on a reach, then center it. Enable automatic weather, follow a front from building wind through rain and clearing skies, and return to the saved manual settings. |
| 8. Harbour capacity | Select a west-quay berth at Ekomarina, back in perpendicular to the quay, moor, walk ashore and leave bow first. Check simultaneous arrivals and 35 usable berths on the connected lake. |

Use the real Niegocin shoreline with daylight terrain, woodlands, fields and shallow-water colours. Retain the sprite-stack art pipeline and use the repository templates for new ImageGen models. The lake chart shows the survey. The scenery and physics share the compact playable projection, with full scale at Ekomarina.
